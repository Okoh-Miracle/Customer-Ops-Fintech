# Project Case Study — Fintech Customer Operations Platform

## The problem

High-volume fintech support becomes difficult to operate when ticket classification, customer context, SLA tracking, knowledge, escalation, and QA live across disconnected tools. Agents can lose time switching systems, while operations leaders lack a single view of queue health and emerging risk.

## The solution

I designed a customer-operations platform that treats support as an operational system rather than a simple inbox. The prototype brings together ticket triage, customer context, SLA exposure, knowledge workflows, escalations, QA, and auditability.

## Core workflow

1. Customer issue enters the support queue.
2. The issue is classified by intent and priority.
3. SLA timing and operational risk determine queue order.
4. The ticket is routed to the appropriate owner.
5. Agents use guided knowledge to resolve common cases.
6. Higher-risk or SLA-sensitive cases are escalated.
7. Resolved interactions enter QA review.
8. Operational actions are recorded in an audit trail.

## Why this matters in fintech

Support operations frequently touch payments, identity verification, account access, refunds, disputes, and transaction questions. Those workflows require clear ownership, consistent policy execution, customer context, and traceability.

## Skills demonstrated

Technical operations, workflow design, customer operations, process engineering, SLA management, data modeling, automation thinking, AI-assisted triage concepts, knowledge management, QA workflows, and audit design.

## Data note

The prototype uses synthetic records for demonstration purposes and contains no real customer or payment information.
